Build target expectation:

Network topology reproduced:
  1. Field scenario:
     - 3 nodes.
     - Node 1: fixed ground destination.
     - Node 2: fixed-wing UAV source, straight 600 m out-and-back path.
     - Node 3: fixed-wing UAV relay, circular path with 30 m radius centered
       around 250 m from Node 1.
     - UAV altitude: about 75 m.
     - Ground height proxy: 10 m.
     - Loop duration: 120 s.

  2. Large scenario:
     - 19 moving UAV nodes.
     - Node 2 scans a rectangular area over 380 s.
     - Nodes 1 and 3..19 are distributed as relay waypoints and circulate
       around their waypoints.
     - Relay circular radius: 30 m.
     - Relay speed: 12 m/s.

Routing protocol logic:
  OLSR baseline:
    Uses ETX-style link cost:
      ETX(route) = sum over hops of 1 / (phi * rho)

  P-OLSR model:
    Uses speed-weighted ETX:
      ETX(hop i,j) = exp(v_ij * beta) / (phi * rho)
    where v_ij is the relative speed estimate between two nodes. Links whose
    endpoints are moving apart get a larger cost; links whose endpoints move
    closer get a smaller cost.

Link-quality and prediction logic:
  - phi and rho are receiving-ratio estimates updated on Hello intervals.
  - Relative speed is estimated from distance change between Hello updates.
  - Relative speed is smoothed with a gamma exponential moving average.

Data flow:
  - Source: Node 2.
  - Destination: Node 1.
  - Offered traffic: 85 UDP datagrams/s, totaling 1 Mbit/s.
  - This reproduction computes expected packet delivery from the paper's
    distance-to-DLR model instead of running a full Wi-Fi packet trace.

Simulation time:
  - Field scenario: 120 s per run.
  - Large scenario: 380 s per run.
  - Default repetitions: 10.

Performance metrics produced:
  - Goodput in Mbit/s.
  - Datagram loss rate (DLR).
  - Packet delivery ratio (PDR).
  - Outage flag and accumulated outage time.
  - Route/path changes as a path-stability proxy.
  - Estimated Hello-message overhead.

Protocol-level reproduction logic:
  - Native ns-3 OLSR remains available as the baseline protocol.
  - The copied local P-OLSR module is built as scratch-polsr-lib.
  - P-OLSR route computation uses a predictive weighted ETX:
      cost(i,j) = exp(relative_speed(i,j) * beta) / (phi(i,j) * rho(i,j))
  - Positions are obtained from ns-3 MobilityModel instances in the experiment.
  - Relative speed is estimated from distance changes and smoothed by gamma.
  - Routes are selected by Dijkstra over symmetric neighbor, two-hop neighbor,
    and topology links.
